Detailed Description

Raised by the relation shortcuts that cannot return a RelationResult - operator->, operator*, begin()/end(), At() and operator[] - when the relation is unavailable.

Prefer the accessors returning RelationResult (Record(), All(), Count(), ...) to handle the unavailable case without exceptions.
